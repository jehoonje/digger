Privacy Policy

Digger · Last updated November 21, 2026

What Digger does

Digger identifies a vinyl record from a photo of its sleeve and plays a matching video from YouTube.

Photos

  • When you take or choose a photo, it is sent to Google Cloud Vision (to find matching images on the web) and to Google Gemini (to read the cover and name the album).
  • Digger does not store your photos. Google processes them under its own terms. Requests to the free tier of the Gemini API may be used by Google to improve its products.
  • The camera is used only while the camera view is open in the app.

Search results

  • To save requests, Digger keeps the YouTube search text (for example, an artist and song name) and the resulting video list for up to 30 days.
  • Digger keeps a short log of each recognition (the album it chose and the titles of matching web pages) to improve accuracy. This log contains no photos and no information about you.
  • Video search uses the YouTube Data API and, when its daily limit is reached, DataForSEO. Videos play in YouTube's embedded player, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

On your device

Your recent scans and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

What Digger does not do

  • No accounts, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information beyond the processing described above.

Children

Digger is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
